Your dog's suite

Each dog gets a private indoor suite and a covered outdoor run, with a cot and fleece blankets ready on arrival.

Special needs and medication

We can care for dogs with certain special needs, special diets and medication. To keep every guest safe, we can't accept dogs with contagious diseases or parasites. Tell us about any changes in your dog's health or normal behavior before the stay.

What to bring

  • A blanket, pillowcase or t-shirt that smells like home
  • Medicine in its original bottle
  • One or two favorite toys. We can't accept rope toys, rawhide or bully sticks. Tennis balls are fine in the yard.
  • Food is included, but bringing food from home avoids a sudden diet change

Before the first stay

  • A passed meet and greet. Book a tour first, and bring your dog on a leash or in a carrier.
  • A signed agreement and an emergency contact, completed once through our online form
  • Vaccine records from a veterinary clinic: rabies, bordetella, distemper and canine influenza
  • A current dog license, where your area requires one

Records are due 48 hours before check-in. See full vaccine timing.

Check-in, pickup and cancellation

  • Checkout is before noon, or before 11 AM on Sundays. Later pickups count as another day of boarding.
  • Drop-off and pickup outside business hours carry a $10 fee plus $1 per minute.
  • Cancellations without notice are charged one night's rate.
